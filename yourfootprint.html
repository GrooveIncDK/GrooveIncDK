www.YourFootPrint.uk is a CO2e calculator and certification system that calculates the CO2e of small farmers and landowners and helps them offset their CO2e emissions with plants and trees. It helps you show your customers how you are working to reduce your CO2e footprint.

Your Footprint gives you an overview of your production. In Your Footprint, you can collect all your products, get direct contact with customers, and show your production's CO2e status, in a new and transparent way.

The world needs to reduce CO2e emissions, and Denmark has a goal of being CO2e neutral from 2050. The people want more solutions and Your Footprint would like to be part of the solution, by contributing with a better overview of one's CO2e emissions, such so that more people can be inspired and gain knowledge to reduce their CO2e footprint.

www.YourFootPrint.uk er en CO2e-beregner og et certificeringssystem, der beregner CO2e for små landmænd og jordejere og hjælper dem med at kompensere deres CO2e-udledning med planter og træer. Det hjælper dig med at vise dine kunder, hvordan du arbejder på at reducere dit CO2e-aftryk.

Your Footprint giver dig et overblik over din produktion. I Your Footprint kan du samle alle dine produkter, få direkte kontakt med kunder og vise din produktions CO2e-status på en ny og gennemsigtig måde.

Verden har brug for at reducere CO2e-udledninger, og Danmark har et mål om at være CO2e-neutral fra 2050. Mennesker ønsker flere løsninger, og Your Footprint vil gerne være en del af løsningen ved at bidrage med et bedre overblik over ens CO2e-udledning, så flere kan blive inspireret og få viden til at reducere deres CO2e-aftryk.

Your Footprint aims to support and involve the farmer/woman in the development of a more sustainable production in relation to the reduction of CO2e, as we provide the opportunity to make calculations on their farm.

Your Footprint contributes to the desire for CO2 neutrality by guiding and nudging the farmers, as our platform offers CO2 eq declaration, which can be used for direct marketing. In this way, it becomes valuable for farmers to restructure their farm so that less CO2e is emitted.

At the same time, we ensure that the farmer has the latest available knowledge at hand, through our mediation, so that the farms can be continuously improved and optimized, in order to provide an even lower CO2e footprint.

======= Your Footprint har til formål at støtte og inddrage landmanden i udviklingen af en mere bæredygtig produktion i forhold til reduktion af CO2e, da vi giver mulighed for at lave beregninger på gården...

Your Footprint har til formål at støtte og inddrage landmanden i udviklingen af en mere bæredygtig produktion i forhold til reduktion af CO2e, da vi giver mulighed for at lave beregninger på gården.

Your Footprint bidrager til ønsket om CO2-neutralitet ved at guide og motivere landmændene, da vores platform tilbyder CO2e-deklaration, som kan bruges til direkte markedsføring. På den måde bliver det værdifuldt for landmænd at omlægge deres gård, så der udledes mindre CO2e.

Samtidig sikrer vi, at landmanden har den nyeste tilgængelige viden ved hånden gennem vores formidling, så gårdene løbende kan forbedres og optimeres for at opnå et endnu lavere CO2e-aftryk.

<<<<<<< HEAD Your Footprints platform has a desire to strengthen biodiversity in Denmark. This happens because the calculations are made based on a balance between grazing and vegetation, so that the ecosystem does not break down and instead flourishes, which can reduce CO2e emissions...

Your Footprints platform has a desire to strengthen biodiversity in Denmark. This happens because the calculations are made based on a balance between grazing and vegetation, so that the ecosystem does not break down and instead flourishes, which can reduce CO2e emissions.

Therefore, Your Footprint is also interested in forestry, as well as private individuals who want to contribute to the green transition. At the same time, we give the farmers and forest owners some more possible sources of income, such as more valuable production and more opportunities for hunting and other leisure activities in the forest.

======= Your Footprints platform har et ønske om at styrke biodiversiteten i Danmark. Det sker, fordi beregningerne laves ud fra en balance mellem græsning og vegetation, så økosystemet ikke bryder sammen, men i stedet blomstrer, hvilket kan reducere CO2e-udledninger...

Your Footprints platform har et ønske om at styrke biodiversiteten i Danmark. Det sker, fordi beregningerne laves ud fra en balance mellem græsning og vegetation, så økosystemet ikke bryder sammen, men i stedet blomstrer, hvilket kan reducere CO2e-udledninger.

Derfor er Your Footprint også interesseret i skovbrug samt privatpersoner, der ønsker at bidrage til den grønne omstilling. Samtidig giver vi landmænd og skovejere flere mulige indtægtskilder, såsom mere værdifuld produktion og flere muligheder for jagt og andre fritidsaktiviteter i skoven.
